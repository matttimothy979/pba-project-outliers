## We plan to have a project that tests whether Taiwanese stocks react to monthly revenue surprises, and whether foreign investors and other institutions trade on that news before it becomes public. It uses free MOPS revenue filings, TWSE daily institutional trading data, and Yahoo Finance prices, and ends with a backtested trading strategy that turns the findings into an investment conclusion.
